# Questions my mom asked

My mom is in her early 70s and curious about everything. Over the past few months she has asked me questions about AI that I could not answer in a sentence. Here are five of them. Pick your own answer to each before you read on.

<GuessCards />

Some of her questions came from things she heard. The first came when I told her that I had used an AI system to build a course for my team at work. One came from a news story. Another came when somebody I know saw a job posting for "AI training", and my mom asked me what that meant.

The words I first used to answer her meant nothing to her: "transformer", "inference" and "harness".

I built this series for her, because neither of us learns much from long explanations. We both learn by seeing a thing and trying it. The series starts before AI, with the first machines that followed instructions, and works up to today's AI systems, one short chapter at a time. Most chapters have something to try with your finger. By the end you will be able to answer all five questions yourself, and chapter 15 shows you your guesses again.

An AI system called Claude wrote every word of this series and every line of its code. Code is the written instructions a computer follows. I told Claude what I wanted, and when it got something wrong, I told it to fix it. Chapter 14 shows how that worked.
