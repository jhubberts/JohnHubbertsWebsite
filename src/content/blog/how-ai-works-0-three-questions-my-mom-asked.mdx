# Three questions my mom asked

A little while ago I was telling my mom how I had used an AI system to build an online course for my team at work. My mom is in her early 70s and curious about everything. She had three questions. One of them came from a news story she had seen.

<GuessCards />

These are good questions. I started to answer them with words like "transformer", "harness" and "container". None of those words meant anything to her.

Neither of us learns much from long explanations. We both learn by seeing a thing and trying it. So I built this series for her: ten short chapters, each with something to try with your finger. By the end you will be able to answer all three questions yourself.

One more thing. I did not write this series. An AI system called Claude wrote every word and every line of code. Code is the written instructions a computer follows. I told it what I wanted, and when it got something wrong, I told it to fix it. The last chapter shows how that worked.
