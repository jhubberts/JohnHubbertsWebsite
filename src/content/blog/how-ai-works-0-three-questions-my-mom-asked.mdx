# Three questions my mom asked

A little while ago I was telling my mom how I had used an AI system to build an online course for my team at work. My mom is in her early 70s and curious about everything. She had three questions.

<GuessCards />

These are good questions. I started to answer them with words like "transformer", "harness" and "container". None of those words meant anything to her.

Neither of us learns much from long explanations. We both learn by seeing a thing and trying it. So I built this for her: ten short chapters, each with something to try with your finger. By the end you will be able to answer all three questions yourself.

One more thing. I did not write this. An AI system called Claude wrote every word and every line of code. I told it what I wanted, and I sent it back when it got something wrong. The last chapter shows how that worked.
