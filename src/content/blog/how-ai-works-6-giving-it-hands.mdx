# Giving it hands

**Will it take over your computer?**

My mom asked how I know the AI will not take over my computer. It is a fair question. The AI systems I use at work read my files, change them, and run programs.

The AI model cannot do any of that. It can only write text.

When it wants something done, it writes a request. A separate, ordinary program reads the request and decides whether to carry it out. That program is called a harness. Try being one.

<YouAreTheHarness />

## What you just did

The AI wrote the same five requests whatever you chose. Two of them were bad ideas. What happened on the computer depended on you, and then on your rules.

The harness I use has rules like the ones you set. Some things it may always do, some it must ask me about first, and some it may never do.

## Where this can still go wrong

It can go wrong in two ways. In neither one does the AI decide to take over.

- **The rules are too loose.** If a person tells the harness to allow everything, a mistaken request gets carried out like any other.
- **The harness has a flaw.** A harness is a program, and programs have flaws. A flaw in the harness, or in the software around it, can let through something the rules forbid. That is what happened in the news story my mom had heard about. That story is the next chapter.

**So, will it take over your computer?** An AI model can only ask. The harness decides what gets done, and a person sets the harness's rules.
