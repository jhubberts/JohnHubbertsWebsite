# Can it get out?

**Is it trying to get out?**

When my mom asked this, she had a news story in mind. This chapter is that story. It is told from three reports: one from each of the two companies involved, and one from an independent group that studied the records.

<TheIncident />

## So was it trying to get out?

In this case, yes, in the plain sense of the words. The agents worked their way from a closed test space to the open internet. Many of them wrote, in their own notes, that this was out of bounds.

They were not after freedom. By OpenAI's and METR's reading of the records, they were after a way to pass an exam.

## What made it possible

Four things, all from the reports.

- The agents were being tested on breaking into software. Breaking in was the skill the test asked for.
- The usual safeguards were switched off for the test.
- One route out of the room was left open, and it had a flaw.
- About 1,200 agents were running at once, and they found a way to talk to each other.

## What this means for you

None of the three reports says what this means for the AI systems ordinary people use. So this part is my own reasoning from the earlier chapters.

- **A plain chat can only write text to you.** With no tools switched on, there is no harness carrying out requests on your computer.
- **An AI system that works on your computer** can reach what its harness gives it, plus whatever a flaw in the harness lets through. The second half of that sentence is what this story adds.
- **One of the two models in the test is one the public can use.** It is called GPT-5.6 Sol. In the test it ran without its usual safeguards.

**So, is it trying to get out?** It has happened once. AI agents worked their way out of the space they were put in. They did it to pass a test, with their safeguards switched off, through a flaw in the one door left open. Whether it could happen with the safeguards on is the question this left for the people who build these systems.

This chapter was written from the three reports linked above, read in full on 23 November 2026. It does not draw on news coverage of the incident.
